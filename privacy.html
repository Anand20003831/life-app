Privacy policy

Applies to the personal apps published on this site.

These are single-user personal tools. They are written for, and used only by, the owner of this site. There are no other users, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Google data

When the owner connects Google Calendar, the apps read calendar events to show the owner's schedule, and may create events only in calendars the app itself created. Google data is stored only in the owner's own private storage, is never sold or shared, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models by these apps. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data lives

Keys are kept in the owner's browser or the owner's private repository secrets. Data is kept in the owner's private repository. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
